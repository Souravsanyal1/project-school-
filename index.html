Craftsmanship & Heritage

Sourced from high-altitude free-roaming herds and master silk weavers during the gentle spring harvest. Each fiber is hand-combed, sorted for supreme fineness, and spun slowly by master artisans in traditional workshops before being loomed into a featherlight yet intensely warm drape.
To maintain the pristine luster of your luxury piece, we recommend gentle professional cleaning with organic solvents only. Store folded in the provided organic cotton dust bag with natural cedar blocks to preserve fiber integrity.
